UFTCL is a securities brokerage and research platform for the Dhaka Stock Exchange (DSE) and Chittagong Stock Exchange (CSE). It combines live prices, charts, volume price analysis and market depth with online trading and BO account services.

Research tools on this site cover DSEX, DSES and DS30, sector and circuit breaker views, IPO tracking, news effect charts and the stock scanner.
