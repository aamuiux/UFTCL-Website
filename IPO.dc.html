What is an IPO?

An initial public offering (IPO) is when a company offers its equity shares to the general public in the open market for the first time. Public subscription is the way a company raises funds from investors. Shares set aside in the offer are reserved for different investor groups, including individual investors, eligible institutions and non-resident Bangladeshis. An IPO is issued either at a fixed price or through book building.
